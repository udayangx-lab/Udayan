## Availability and pricing

The source’s separate Tarakeswar carousel mixes upcoming and current movies, while several movie links contain the old date 20260812. Do not interpret these as verified current showtimes. The new site uses undated venue URLs:

- https://in.bookmyshow.com/cinemas/hooghly/udayan-cinema-sheoraphuli/buytickets/UCPL/
- https://in.bookmyshow.com/cinemas/tarkeshwar/udayan-cinema-tarkeshwar/buytickets/UUCT/

Ticket prices, available seats, actual showtimes, ratings and durations are not exposed reliably by the public content and are not fabricated. Visitors check BookMyShow.

## Facilities

2K laser projection, Dolby 7.1 surround sound, premium recliner seating, food and beverages, and DPD Lounge & Café. Two-wheeler parking at Sheoraphuli; two- and four-wheeler parking at Tarakeswar. Seating class and availability must be checked for the selected show.
