Testimonial

Udayan Cinema in Sheoraphuli offers one of the best movie-viewing experiences in the area. The theater stands out not only for its diverse seating options, including Gold, Silver, Royal Recliner, and luxurious sofas, but also for the comfort and ambiance it provides. Personally, I find the Royal Recliner to be the best choice, as it enhances the overall experience with its plush seating and perfect viewing angle. The staff at Udayan Cinema are notably polite, adding to the positive experience. They are always ready to assist, making the entire visit pleasant from start to finish. While the picture and audio quality may not be the absolute best compared to some high-end theaters, they are still good enough to ensure a satisfying movie experience. The quality is more than adequate for enjoying a quality time with friends or family. The cinema also offers a variety of food and beverages at affordable prices. The food is tasty and of good quality, although I believe the experience could be further enhanced by expanding the menu, especially by adding more beverage options. Booking tickets is convenient, with the option to use the BookMyShow app or purchase them offline at the theater. This flexibility ensures that everyone can easily secure their preferred seats. Overall, Udayan Cinema is a great place to enjoy movies, with its comfortable seating, friendly staff, and decent food options. With a few improvements, it could easily become the go-to spot for movie lovers in Sheoraphuli.

Pritam Kumar

This is a popular cinema hall near Sheoraphuli station, beside the G.T.Road. 1. Heritage & Location: It’s one of the older cinemas around (established 1946). Very conveniently located: near Sheoraphuli railway station and on G.T.Road - Makes it accessible. 2. Facilities after renovation / upgrades: One screen has been renovated to a plex model, with 2K projection, 3D silver screen. Air conditioning is present. Comfortable seating arrangements. Ticket prices are reasonable for what’s offered. 3. Show variety & experience: They screen both Bengali and Hindi films - Audience variety. The sound system and cozy environment in certain shows/screens is noteworthy.

A Fantastic Cinema Experience with Great Games and Grub! I recently visited and was thoroughly impressed! But what really sets this place apart is its accompanying cafe.
